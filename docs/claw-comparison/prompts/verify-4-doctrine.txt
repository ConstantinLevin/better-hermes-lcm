You are auditing "betterlcm" (a fork of the hermes-lcm plugin, Python, at the repository root) against ONE rule, exhaustively.

THE RULE: nothing may be lost or hidden without a marker. Concretely, every one of these is a violation:
 (a) content that reaches the plugin and then becomes unreachable — not stored, deleted, overwritten, or reachable from no summary node;
 (b) content removed from what the summariser reads, or from what the agent reads back, with nothing in its place saying it was removed and how to get it;
 (c) a bounded, capped, timed-out, failed or partial operation whose result is presented as complete (an empty result that reads as "there is nothing", a summary that reads as covering everything, a status that reads as healthy);
 (d) a summary or index whose provenance is missing, wrong, or unresolvable;
 (e) a marker that can itself be removed, trimmed away, or that says less than what was removed.
Summaries themselves are NOT violations: they are acceptable because they are marked, carry provenance and act as an index.
